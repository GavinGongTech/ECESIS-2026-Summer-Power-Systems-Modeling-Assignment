*Companion file to BACKLOG_LITERATURE.md. Originally tracked the full reference list of each L5-referenced paper assessed so we could cross-examine at the end of the batch for foundational works cited by ≥2 of them. After the L5 and L12 anchor-tier rewrites from PDF in the current revision, this file now also captures L5's own 40-reference bibliography and L12's own 26-reference bibliography as primary sources, since both anchor papers are now directly assessed at full PhD-level depth.*

---

## Scope and methodology

**What this file tracks:** Initially designed to walk through L5's (Triebe et al. 2025) reference list, with each paper we assessed having its own reference list recorded here. After the L5/L12 anchor-tier rewrites, the file also captures L5's and L12's own bibliographies as primary sources. At the end of the batch, we run frequency analysis: any reference cited by ≥2 of L5's referenced papers (plus L5 and L12 themselves) becomes a candidate for direct assessment.

**Cross-reference convention:** When a referenced paper appears already in our L1–L41 backlog, it gets marked `[ALREADY IN BACKLOG: Lxx]`. When it's a candidate for assessment based on multi-citation frequency, it gets marked `[FREQ ≥2 — candidate for direct assessment]` in the frequency tally section.

**Recording detail:** For each paper, record the full reference list with author(s), year, title, and venue. Capture enough to deduplicate across papers (same paper cited slightly differently in different bibliographies — e.g. Sevlian & Rajagopal 2018 IJEPES vs. Rajagopal & Sevlian 2018 — should match).

**Self-citations and trivial refs:** Authors' own prior work is recorded but flagged as `[self-cite]` since multi-citation by the same author group is less informative than multi-citation across independent groups.
